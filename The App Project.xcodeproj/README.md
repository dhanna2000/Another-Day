# Another Day

## Purpose

Another Day helps you plan your day, build habits, and reflect each week. It combines a simple to-do list with habit scheduling (by weekday and time of day) and a weekly dashboard that summarizes your progress. The app can read Health data (water intake and exercise minutes) to include in your weekly snapshot. All data is stored locally on your device.
